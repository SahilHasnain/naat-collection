2. Information We Collect

4. Data Storage

Playback history, preferences, and downloaded media are stored locally on your device. If you use Google sign-in or favorites synchronization, the related account and favorites data is processed and stored by Appwrite for the app. In app versions with diagnostics enabled and configured, technical diagnostic information may be sent to Sentry. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete information already stored by these services.

7. Children's Privacy
